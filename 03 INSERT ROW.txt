To insert row into a table
(comma separated data for each coloumn)
statement:
INSERT INTO tablename
VALUES(,);

example:
INSERT INTO employees
VALUES (1,"Eugene","Krabs",25.50,"2023-01-02");
SELECT * FROM employees;

Inserting mutliple rows at once:
INSERT INTO tablename
VALUES(),(),(),();
